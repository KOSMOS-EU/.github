# KOSMOS

**An inter-municipal exchange format curated by the City of Brandis.**

KOSMOS is hosted by the City of Brandis (Saxony, Germany) and intentionally
established as a neutral platform — not as a Brandis-branded initiative. Its
purpose is to enable open, vendor-neutral exchange among small urban authorities
and municipal software providers.

Trägerschaft (institutional sponsorship) lies with the Saxon municipality of
[Brandis](https://stadt-brandis.de/).

## Focus

- **Sustainability of public digital infrastructure** — keeping municipal IT
  maintainable, transferable, and independent over the long term.
- **Technical convergence** between open-source and commercial offerings.
- **Integration over lock-in** — providing pathways for commercial providers to
  integrate into public infrastructure rather than impose proprietary lock-ins.

## Why neutral?

KOSMOS is deliberately not a single municipality's brand. Brandis acts as host
and curator, but the platform belongs to its participants. This neutrality is
what allows small urban authorities and competing software vendors to
collaborate on shared infrastructure on equal terms.

## Projects

Beyond exchange, KOSMOS is where concrete projects are initiated — all guided by
the goal of converging a fragmented municipal digital landscape into a coherent
one. These typically take one of three forms:

- **Adapters** — connecting existing proprietary software to open municipal
  infrastructure.
- **Intermediate layers** — shared components that sit between systems and let
  them interoperate.
- **Implementations** — translating established as well as new, modern concepts
  into running municipal solutions.

## Guiding ideals

Brandis sees itself in the ideals of a forward-looking municipality — and
borrows openly from the criteria the EU sets for progressive public
administration: **open, service-oriented, sustainable, and proactive**.
In particular, KOSMOS projects aim to be:

- **user-centric** — designed around the needs of citizens and staff, not
  around systems.
- **interoperable by default** — built to connect and exchange data across
  authorities and providers from the start.
- **once-only** — following the once-only principle, so citizens and businesses
  provide the same information to the administration only once.

KOSMOS exists to put these principles into practice across municipal
boundaries.

## Participants

- Municipal administrations from across Saxony, including but not limited to the
  Partheland region.
- Commercial municipal software providers.

## About Brandis

Brandis is a small town in the [Partheland](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parthe),
a network of municipalities east of Leipzig, Saxony. It sees itself as one of
the digitalisation drivers in the region: Brandis was named Saxony's first
*Innovationskommune* (2014–2016) and went on to lead a federal Smart-Cities
model project extending shared digital infrastructure across several Partheland
communities — always with the requirement that solutions be transferable to
other municipalities. KOSMOS continues that line: build good things once, then
share them openly.
